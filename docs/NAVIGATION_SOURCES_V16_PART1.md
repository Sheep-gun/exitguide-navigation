# Navigation V16 official-source verification — Part 1

Verification date: **2026-07-30**
Proposal inspected: `docs/NAVIGATION_COVERAGE_GAPS_V16.md`, domains **1–6 only**
Jurisdiction: United States federal sources unless a row says otherwise

## Scope and decision rules

This audit retrieved every HTTPS candidate in V16 domains 1–6 and recorded the observed destination, redirect or retrieval failure. It did not inspect any independent fixture, answer, failure, evaluation, or generator surface. It did not modify the V16 proposal, the canonical catalog, source modules, fixtures, or tests.

**Every candidate row below was verified on 2026-07-30.** The single date applies per row and is stated once here to avoid repeating an identical date in all 40 rows.

Decision terms:

- `accepted`: the URL is currently retrievable (possibly after a same-authority redirect), belongs to the issuing authority or an official federal regulatory repository, and supports at least the stated terminal or scope.
- `replaced`: the candidate was blocked, empty, stale, or otherwise unusable as evidence; the row supplies a retrieved equal-or-better official replacement.
- `rejected`: no suitable evidentiary use or official replacement was established.
- `sufficient`: the source identifies the regulated actor/role, governed asset, and lifecycle duty or transition needed by the mapped terminal cluster.
- `partial`: useful only as an operational endpoint, vocabulary source, or one half of a rule-plus-portal pair. It must not be the sole source for a consequential terminal.

The eCFR is an official U.S. government regulatory repository and labels its continuously updated text “authoritative but unofficial.” It is accepted here as the current codified-rule surface, while the issuing agency remains the program authority. An accepted source does **not** prove that a particular commercial product exposes a matching screen.

Terminal suffixes below are exact when joined to the domain prefix declared at the start of each section.
